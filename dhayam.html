Dhayam Sandbox Rules

This is a free-movement sandbox board. Players act as referees to implement traditional tactical rules using the sandbox tools and rollback states.

  • 1) First Onboarding (Dhayam): You must roll a strict 1 (Dhayam) to active your very first coin out onto the board. Until then, pass your turn.
  • 2) Yard Spawn Constraints: A 1 or 5 can only be spent to move coins already active on the grid if your starting Yard is completely empty. If coins are in the yard, 1 or 5 must be used to onboard them.
  • 3) Inner Spiral Entry Cut: You are forbidden from turning off the outer loop into the inner gateway lanes leading to the center goal unless your color has cut at least one opponent coin.
  • 4) Complete Bank Movement: Every turn, you must strategically use **all values sitting in your active roll bank** (including all 1, 5, 6, 12 bonuses). Rolls cannot be discarded.
  • 5) Cut Avoidance Rollback: If a path choice lands you in an unwanted trap, forces an unwanted cut, or leaves you stuck with illegal leftover bank numbers, use **"Restore Bank Snapshot"** to rewind your entire turn and pick a completely different path.
