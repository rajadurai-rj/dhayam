Dhayam Traditional Rules & Instructions ▼

Welcome to the Dhayam Sandbox. This traditional Indian board game is played on a 7x7 grid. The objective is to navigate all your pieces from your starting courtyard along the outer loop, loop into the inner lanes, and successfully land them into the center GOAL box.

  • Entering the Board: A piece can only leave your personal yard and enter active board play when you roll a 1 (Dhayam) or a 5.
  • Rolling Dice: Click "Roll Strategy Dice" to generate steps. Rolling a 1, 5, 6, or 12 grants you an extra bonus roll. All rolls accumulate into your Active Roll Bank.
  • Executing Movements: Select a token, then click a target cell to move it by clicking/spending an item from your roll bank.
  • Safe Havens (Malai ✕): The 8 marked cross cells are standard safe zones. Opposite player tokens can sit in the same safe haven without cutting each other.
  • Cutting Opponents: Landing your token on a normal square occupied by a different player cuts their token, instantly sending it back to their starting yard.
  • Snapshot Recovery: If a mistake or accidental misclick happens during gameplay, use the "Restore Bank Snapshot" feature to instantly revert the bank and board to the previous turn state.
